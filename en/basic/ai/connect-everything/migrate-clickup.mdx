Move existing ClickUp work into a live Teable project management system. Teable AI can turn exported Spaces, Folders, Lists, tasks, and subtasks into related projects, work items, owners, statuses, dates, custom fields, and dependencies without rebuilding every record by hand.

## Export ClickUp task data

In ClickUp, open **Workspace settings → Imports / Exports → Export Items**, choose the Spaces, Folders, or Lists to include, and download the CSV. ClickUp documents the current steps and included fields in [Export task data](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/6310551109527-Export-task-data).

## Migrate with Teable AI

1. Open [Teable AI Chat](/en/basic/ai/ai-chat) in the target base.
2. Attach the ClickUp CSV, or type `/` and select **Connect Everything** to use an authorized connection.
3. Ask Teable AI to migrate the complete hierarchy and create the views, dashboards, and automations your team needs.

> Migrate this ClickUp workspace into related Spaces, Projects, Lists, Work Items, and People. Preserve tasks, subtasks, owners, dates, statuses, custom fields, priorities, comments, and attachment links. Build a live project dashboard and flag workload or schedule risk.

Teable AI can build the supported records, fields, relationships, views, dashboards, and workflows from the project data you provide.

<Note>
Available data depends on the ClickUp export or API permissions. Docs, dashboards, automations, permissions, time entries, and some attachments may need separate validation.
</Note>
