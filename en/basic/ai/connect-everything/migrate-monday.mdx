Move existing monday.com boards into a live Teable project management system. Teable AI can turn exported boards into related projects, work items, owners, statuses, dates, custom fields, dependencies, and updates without rebuilding every board by hand.

## Export a monday.com board

Open the board menu, choose **More actions → Export board to Excel**, and select the data to include. monday.com documents the current options and export limitations in [Export from monday to Excel](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/26989749858578-Export-from-monday-to-Excel).

## Migrate with Teable AI

1. Open [Teable AI Chat](/en/basic/ai/ai-chat) in the target base.
2. Attach the exported Excel file, or type `/` and select **Connect Everything** to use an authorized connection.
3. Ask Teable AI to migrate the complete board structure and create the views, dashboards, and automations your team needs.

> Migrate these monday.com boards into related Projects, Work Items, People, Statuses, and Groups. Preserve items, subitems, owners, dates, column values, dependencies, and included updates. Build a live portfolio dashboard and flag blocked or overdue work.

Teable AI can build the supported records, fields, relationships, views, dashboards, and workflows from the project data you provide.

<Note>
Available data depends on the monday.com export or API permissions. Dashboards, automation recipes, integrations, apps, permissions, and private-board content may need separate validation.
</Note>
