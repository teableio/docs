<Tip>Available for self-hosted Business plan and above</Tip>

Click **Create space** and enter a space name to create a new space directly.

### Space Information

* **Space name**: The display name of the space
* **Project count**: The total number of projects contained in the space
* **Collaborator count**: The total number of users who have access to the space
* **Created time**: When the space was created
* **Public space**: Whether newly registered users join the space automatically
* **Concurrency**: The maximum number of AI field generations and workflow runs that can run at the same time in the space, shown as "AI field generations / workflow runs"

#### Auto-join

* **Allow auto join**: When enabled, all newly registered users will automatically join this space with [Read permission](/en/basic/space/space-invite). Useful for public or company-wide spaces.
* **Disallow auto join**: Disable automatic joining for new users.

#### Delete Space

<Warning>Spaces deleted from System Admin **cannot be recovered**. Unlike regular space deletion, spaces deleted here do not enter the trash bin system.</Warning>

When you delete a space from System Admin:
* The space is immediately marked as deleted
* All projects and data within the space become inaccessible
* Collaborators lose access to the space
* This operation cannot be undone

**Before deleting a space:**
1. Notify all collaborators about the deletion
2. Export or backup important data
3. Confirm that the space is no longer needed
