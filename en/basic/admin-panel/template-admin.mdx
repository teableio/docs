### Template Management

Templates come from project submissions. A member submits a project for review with **Publish to Template Center**, and once an admin approves it in **Template review**, the template is listed and appears in the template center. Here, admins can adjust template details, control whether templates are listed, and change their order.

#### Template Information

* **Cover**: The cover image for the template card
* **Name** and **Description**: The title and short description displayed in the template center
* **Markdown description**: Detailed description supporting Markdown formatting
* **Industry**: Group templates by industry and sub-industry, or by team. Click the settings icon next to the column header to open **Manage industries and teams**, where you can add industries, sub-industries, and teams
* **URL slug**: The identifier in the template's public page URL. Use only lowercase letters, digits, and hyphens
* **Featured**: Feature the template in the template center. Available only after the template is published
* **Integrations**: The platforms the template's automations need to connect to. After installing, users are guided through connecting them
* **Snapshot Time**: When the template content was last approved
* **Created by**: The user who published the template
* **Usage/Visit**: How many times the template has been used and visited

#### Template Operations

* **Published**: Toggle to list or unlist the template in the template center. Requires a snapshot, a name, and a description.
* **Preview**: Open the template in a new tab
* **Pin top**: Move the template to the top of the list. You can also drag the handle at the start of a row to reorder templates
* **Delete**: Remove the template

To update a template's content, open **Publish to Template Center** again in the source project and click **Submit v\{version\} for review**. Until the new version is approved, anyone who installs the template still gets the current version.

### Review Templates

Open **System Admin** > **Template review** and use the **Review queue** to see pending submissions with their author, submission time, and automatic check results. The checks are reminders only and do not block a decision: **Connections** flags integrations the template uses that this deployment cannot connect to; **Privacy** flags that the template includes records, so make sure they hold no personal data; **Run estimate** flags that monthly runs may exceed the free plan; **Trial run** flags that the automations need no account, so confirm they run without one. Click **View** to open the submission details, preview the frozen copy, and choose an industry for the template.

* **Approve**: What goes live is the copy frozen when the author submitted. Each approved update to a listed template raises its version by one. Later edits by the author need a new submission
* **Reject**: The author gets your note and can submit again after making changes

You can add a note with either decision, and the author is notified of the result.

### Using Official Templates

Self-hosted users can import templates from the official Teable Cloud:

1. **Download**: Get the Teable project export file (`.tea`) from the [Official Template Center](https://teable.ai/templates)
2. **Import**: Import the `.tea` file as a new project in your space
3. **Open the publish dialog**: In the imported project, click the project name in the top-left corner and select **Publish to Template Center**
4. **Fill in details**: Enter a name and description, choose what it includes and whether to include sample records, and upload a cover if needed
5. **Submit and approve**: Click **Submit for review**, then click **Approve** in **System Admin** > **Template review**. The template is listed right away and appears in **System Admin** > **Template admin**
