## Overview

In Teable Cloud, enter the target space:

1. Click `···` in the upper-right corner of the space
2. Open **Space Settings**
3. Go to **Authentication**
4. Verify at least one of your organization's email domains under **Verified domains**
5. Add or manage an SSO provider

<Info>SSO only lets users whose email belongs to a verified domain sign in, and only those users see the SSO button on the sign-in page. You can't add a provider until a domain is verified, and existing providers can't sign anyone in until you verify one.</Info>

## How It Works

All integrations use the **OpenID Connect (OIDC)** protocol based on OAuth 2.0:

1. User clicks SSO login on Teable
2. User is redirected to your identity provider
3. User authenticates with their credentials
4. Identity provider sends user information back to Teable
5. User is logged into Teable
