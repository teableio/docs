## Teable is now on the App Store and Google Play {#release-2026-10-30-section-1}

**Work, wherever. With Teable.**

Teable is now available on iPhone, iPad and Android. Whenever work comes up, whether you're at home, in a café, on a train or in another city, your projects, tables, apps and chats are right there. So is Cuppy, Teable's AI agent.

Whether you run a gym, a restaurant, an online store or your team's operations on Teable, you can now keep it running from your phone.

### 1\. Pick up where you left off {#release-2026-10-30-section-2}

Start a chat with Cuppy at your desk and finish it on your phone. No syncing, no setup.

### 2\. Say it, and Cuppy does it {#release-2026-10-30-section-3}

Ask Cuppy what needs your attention today, or have it update records, write a report or summarize a file. Type or hold to talk, and attach a photo, PDF or spreadsheet when you need to.

### 3\. Build and update apps on the go {#release-2026-10-30-section-4}

Describe the app or page you need, preview it, then publish it and share the link from your phone.

On iPad or an unfolded iPhone Duo, Teable splits into two columns, with your projects on the left and your work on the right. Open a table from a chat, and the chat stays right beside it.

**[Get the Teable app](https://teable.ai/mobile?ref=changelog)**. Free on iPhone, iPad and Android. Sign in with your Teable account. New to Teable? Sign up at teable.ai first, then sign in on your phone.

## Teable is now GDPR compliant {#release-2026-10-27-section-1}

Your business data deserves clear protection. Whether you store customer details, contact lists, or employee records in Teable, here's how we protect it:

![Teable is now GDPR compliant](https://dxshyegpql0u3hra.public.blob.vercel-storage.com/c7c2b80bbeaf138d4ec2a0a4baa95ba9.png)

- **A DPA for every hosted workspace.** Our Data Processing Addendum covers GDPR, UK GDPR, and Swiss FADP, with EU Standard Contractual Clauses included. It applies without a separate signature. [Read the DPA](https://teable.ai/dpa).
- **A representative in Europe.** We've appointed DataRep as our representative in the EU, the UK, and Switzerland, giving you a direct contact for data protection inquiries.
- **Your data is not used to train AI models.** On Teable Cloud, our AI providers process your content to complete your requests and are contractually prohibited from using it for training.
- **Transparency on subprocessors.** Our subprocessor list is public, with 30 days' notice before we add a new one.
- **Self-hosting for control over data storage.** Run Teable on your own servers and keep your database on your infrastructure.

**SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO 27001 are underway.** Follow our progress and find security and compliance information at the [Teable Trust Center](https://trust.teable.ai).

</Update>

## Connect AI tools to Teable with MCP {#release-2026-10-20-section-1}

Teable now has an official MCP server. Connect **Claude Code, Codex, Cursor**, or any other MCP client, and work with your Teable data through conversation. For example, ask it to check what shipped this week in your repo and update your roadmap in Teable. It can query and update records, and create tables, fields, and views, all within your own Teable permissions.

<video controls playsInline preload="auto" className="w-full aspect-video" poster="https://dxshyegpql0u3hra.public.blob.vercel-storage.com/38ab53314236be0c7e63a70c8736ab10.png" src="https://media-static.teable.ai/video/1571b575ae42e0a9.mp4" aria-label="Connect AI tools to Teable with MCP" />

<span id="how-to-connect" />

### How to connect {#release-2026-10-20-section-2}

No API token needed. In Claude Code, run:

```bash
claude mcp add --transport http teable https://mcp.teable.ai/mcp
```

Then run `/mcp`, choose **teable → Authenticate**, and approve access in your browser.

In Codex CLI, run:

```bash
codex mcp add teable --url https://mcp.teable.ai/mcp
```

Then run `codex mcp login teable` and approve access in your browser.

For Cursor and other clients, open **Settings → MCP** in Teable for copy-ready setup steps. You can also see connected clients there and remove them at any time.

</Update>
