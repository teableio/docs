When AI builds a chart, report, dashboard-style page, or a small tool such as a calculator in a conversation, it saves the result as an **artifact**: a page that renders on its own outside the conversation, either an interactive HTML page or a Markdown report. Artifacts appear as a card in the chat and stay available after the conversation ends.

To create one, describe the result you want in [AI Chat](/en/basic/ai/ai-chat), for example "Generate a weekly sales trend chart" or "Create a summary report from these records." Artifacts created during a [routine](/en/basic/ai/routine) run belong to that run's owner.

<Info>
An artifact holds the data AI put into it when it was created, so it does not refresh on its own, and the page cannot read or change your tables. For a page that keeps reading live table data, or that collects input and stores state, create an app with [App Builder](/en/basic/ai/app-builder).
</Info>

## View and Revise

Click the card in the chat to open the artifact viewer. From the viewer you can:

- **Fullscreen** or **Open in new page** to give the artifact more room.
- **Download** the version you are viewing: an HTML page downloads as an `.html` file and a Markdown report as an `.md` file.
- Open the version list to look at earlier versions.
- **Delete** the artifact. Deletion cannot be undone: its cards in the conversation turn into a deleted placeholder, and its share link stops working.

To change an artifact, ask AI in the same conversation. Each revision becomes a new version of the same artifact rather than a separate one, so the share link keeps working. Open an earlier version and click **Restore this version** to add a new version with its content; the versions after it are kept.

If the page fails to run, the viewer shows the error at the top. Click **Fix with AI**, and Teable starts a new chat in the artifact's own project and sends the error to AI. Click **View** in the notice to open the repair chat and follow its progress.

## Share an Artifact

By default only the person who created an artifact can view it; other members of the same project cannot see it either. To let anyone else view it, turn on sharing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on sharing">
    Click **Share** in the viewer and turn on **Enable sharing**. Others can then open the artifact through its link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can view">
    **Who can view** defaults to **Anyone with the link**, which opens without signing in. Choose **Space members** so that only signed-in members of the space or of the project can open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a password (optional)">
    Under **Advanced**, turn on **Password protection**. Teable generates a password automatically, and viewers must enter it before the artifact loads. The password is shown only right after it is generated, so click **Copy link and password** then to copy both together. Later you can click **Set Password** to choose or change the password; after a change, viewers need the new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the link">
    Click the **Copy link** button next to the link. If the link has spread too far, turn off **Enable sharing** and the link stops working for others. Turning sharing back on restores the same link with its previous viewer and password settings, so to keep out people who already have the link, switch to **Space members** right after turning it back on, or turn on **Password protection** and change the password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

People who open the link always see the latest version and cannot see earlier versions. Once sharing is on, you can also embed the link in another website with an iframe.

For examples of this feature in use, read [shareable AI report examples](https://teable.ai/blog/introducing-artifacts-share-data-reports-anywhere).

## Find Artifacts Later

Open your avatar at the bottom left → **Settings** → **Manage artifacts**, or open it from a chat through **+** → **More** → **Manage artifacts**.

- **Mine**: The artifacts you created, shown as cards grouped by project, each with a thumbnail of the page's first screen at the top. You can search by name. A share icon next to the name marks artifacts with sharing on. Use a card's **...** menu to **Share**, **Download**, **Open in new page**, or **Delete** it, or click the card to open the viewer.
- **Shared with me**: Artifacts that others shared and you opened while signed in, with who shared them and when you last opened them. Click one to reopen it in a new tab. Once sharing is turned off or the artifact is deleted, its entry no longer appears.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion id="artifact-sharing-faq" title="Can my teammates open an artifact I created?">
    Not by default, even if they are in the same project. Turn on **Enable sharing** in the viewer and send them the link, choosing **Space members** if the artifact should stay inside the space.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do artifacts differ from apps built with App Builder?">
    An artifact is a snapshot of the data at the time AI created it, suited to one-off reports, charts, and analysis results. An app reads and writes your tables live, can collect input and store state, and suits tools and pages you will keep using.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do artifacts use credits?">
    Creating and revising an artifact is part of an AI Chat conversation, so it counts against credits based on the conversation's actual usage. Viewing, sharing, and downloading use no credits.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
