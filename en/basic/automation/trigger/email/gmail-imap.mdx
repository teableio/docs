## Prepare Gmail for email automation {#gmail-setup-section-1}

This guide prepares your Gmail account for the email-received trigger in Teable.

<span id="enable-imap-in-gmail" />

### Check IMAP access {#gmail-setup-section-2}

- **Personal Gmail:** IMAP is always enabled. Since January 2025, Gmail no longer shows an “Enable IMAP” switch. See [Google’s Gmail setup guide](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/7126229).
- **Google Workspace:** Ask your administrator to confirm that IMAP access and the connection method are allowed for your account. Administrators manage IMAP access and permitted clients in the [Google Admin console](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/sync/turn-pop-and-imap-on-or-off-for-users).

<span id="enable-2-step-verification" />

### Enable 2-Step Verification {#gmail-setup-section-3}

1. Open your [Google Account security settings](https://myaccount.google.com/security) and sign in to the Gmail account you want to connect.
2. Open **2-Step Verification** and follow Google’s steps to enable it.

<span id="create-app-password" />

### Create an app password {#gmail-setup-section-4}

1. Open [App passwords](https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords). Google may ask you to sign in again.
2. Follow the prompts to create an app password for `Teable IMAP`.
3. Copy the generated **16-character app password** and use it as the password for the Gmail connection in Teable.

If the option is missing, check that 2-Step Verification is enabled. App passwords may be unavailable for accounts using only security keys for verification, accounts enrolled in Advanced Protection, or work and school accounts. For a managed account, ask your administrator whether app passwords are permitted. See [Google’s app password guidance](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185833).

<span id="notes" />

### Notes {#gmail-setup-section-5}

- Use the app password in Teable. Your regular Google Account password cannot replace it.
- Save the app password securely: Google only displays it once. Revoke it when you no longer use the connection.
- Changing your Google Account password revokes existing app passwords. Create a new one and update the connection in Teable.
- Only new, unread emails trigger the automation.
