Form views are generated from the current table. Each submission creates a new record in the table where the form view was created. Use forms to collect feedback, registrations, reimbursement requests, customer details, and other structured data.

## Use Cases

| Scenario | Good for |
| --- | --- |
| Information collection | Customer details, registration forms, surveys, feedback |
| Internal requests | Reimbursements, purchasing, leave requests, access requests |
| File submission | Resumes, receipts, contracts, screenshots |
| Lightweight workflow entry | Save external submissions directly as table records |

## Create a Form View

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the target table">
    Go to the table that should store form submissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a form view">
    Open the view sidebar, click `+`, and choose **Form view**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the name">
    Enter a form view name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the view">
    After creating the view, Teable opens the form builder for field setup.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configure Form Fields

Form fields are generated from the table fields. In the form builder, you can adjust what submitters see.

<img
  src="/images/docs/base/2026-10-21-view-form-builder.png"
  alt="Form builder with the Fields sidebar on the left and the form cover, title, fields, and Submit button on the right"
  className="docs-screenshot"
/>

| Action | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Reorder fields | Drag fields on the form to change their order |
| Hide fields | Click the hide button (**Hide from the form**) on the right of a field, or drag the field to the **Drag field here to hide from form** area on the left |
| Add fields | Click a field under **Fields** on the left, or drag it onto the form. Click **Create field** to add a new field to the table |
| Rename field | Click a field on the form to open its field settings and rename it. The name updates in both the table and the form |
| Field description | Add a description in the field settings. It appears below the field name on the form to explain what to enter |
| Require a field | Turn on the **Required** switch on the right of a field so the form cannot be submitted without it |

## Form Appearance

Form appearance supports a cover image, logo, and submit button text. The cover area uses a fixed height, so avoid placing important text or key information in an image that may be cropped.

## Share the Form

After configuring the form, click **Share** on the right side of the form view toolbar, turn on **Share view to web**, then copy the form link and send it to submitters.

To see the page submitters see, switch to **Fill** on the left side of the toolbar. Switch back to **Edit** to keep configuring the form. Submissions made in **Fill** are also saved as new records in the table.

## Submission Settings

After sharing is on, set submission restrictions under **Advanced** in the share dialog.

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Restrict by password | Generates a password when turned on. You can copy the link and password together or change the password. Submitters must enter the correct password before they can open and submit the form |
| Require login submit | Requires submitters to log in to Teable before submitting. The submitter is recorded as the record creator, which you can see in a Created By field |

When **Require login submit** is on, visitors who are not logged in must log in or create a Teable account before submitting.
